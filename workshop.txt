title=Car Claim System
description=[h1]Car Claim System[/h1]
description=[h2]Your car, your rules[/h2]
description=[b]Claim a vehicle, then decide who may ride in it, drive it, open its storage, work on it or tow it.[/b]
description=
description=Right-click a vehicle and open [b]Vehicle Claim[/b]. Claiming needs the vehicle's key (or a hotwire) and you standing next to it.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]What a Claim Stops[/h2]
description=Without your say-so, nobody can:
description=[list]
description=[*] get in, or take the driver's seat
description=[*] open the trunk, glovebox or seat storage
description=[*] remove, install or repair parts, let the tyres down or siphon the tank
description=[*] hotwire it, smash its windows or work its locks
description=[*] hitch it to anything, or unhitch it
description=[/list]
description=
description=Anyone can still put fuel in. Burnt and smashed wrecks cannot be claimed, so nobody can claim a wreck to block a road.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]Five Separate Permissions[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Passenger[/b] - get in, use the doors, locks and windows
description=[*] [b]Drive[/b] - the driver's seat and the engine
description=[*] [b]Storage[/b] - the trunk and every container
description=[*] [b]Mechanics[/b] - parts, repairs, tyres, the hood, siphoning
description=[*] [b]Tow[/b] - hitching and unhitching
description=[/list]
description=
description=Each one is its own tick. Letting a friend ride along does not let them strip the engine.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]Who Gets Them[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]My Faction[/b] and [b]My Safehouse[/b] - follow who is in the group right now. Someone who joins gets access, someone kicked loses it at once
description=[*] [b]Nearby Players[/b] - pick someone standing within ten tiles
description=[*] [b]Online Players[/b] - pick anyone connected
description=[/list]
description=
description=A player's own entry overrides the groups, so one faction member can be kept out of a car the rest can use. [b]View Permissions[/b] shows everyone with access in one table, online or not.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]Passing It On[/h2]
description=[b]Transfer Ownership[/b] sends an offer to an online player, who has a minute to accept. The permission list is wiped when the car changes hands. Claims belong to the account, so they survive your character dying.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]For Server Owners[/h2]
description=Three sandbox options:
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Claims Per Player[/b] - default 3
description=[*] [b]Claim Expiry[/b] - a claim lapses when its owner has not logged in for this many real days, default 30
description=[*] [b]Idle Engine Shut-off[/b] - any vehicle left running with nobody in the driver's seat is switched off after this many real minutes, default 10
description=[/list]
description=Set any of them to 0 to turn it off.
description=
description=Admins pass every check, and can release a claim or hand it to someone else from the same menu. Both are written to the server log.
description=
description=[b]The server decides.[/b] Claims are kept by the server, not on the vehicle. Parts, fuel, locks and hotwiring are refused server-side. Somebody sitting where they may not has the engine cut and is put out once the car stops, and a hitch that was not allowed is undone.
description=
